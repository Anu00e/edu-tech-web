About Cognira Edutech

Cognira Edutech is an education technology company focused on empowering students with meaningful learning, informed career guidance, and technology-enabled education solutions.

We work with school students, college learners, and aspiring professionals to bridge the gap between academics, skills, and career readiness.

Cognira is a Udyam-registered and GST-compliant company, operating in accordance with Indian business and education standards.

As part of our innovation roadmap, we are developing Askly — an AI-powered learning initiative designed to support clearer understanding and smarter learning experiences (coming soon).
