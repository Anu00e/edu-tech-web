Cognira Scholarship Programs

Supporting potential, rewarding effort, and enabling access to quality education.

At Cognira Edutech, we believe that financial limitations should never restrict learning potential. Our scholarship initiatives are thoughtfully designed to identify deserving students and provide them with structured academic support, skill development opportunities, and guided learning pathways.

Cognira scholarships focus not only on academic performance, but also on curiosity, consistency, problem-solving ability, and career ambition. Through these programs, we aim to empower school and college students to learn with confidence, clarity, and purpose.

STEM & Academics

Academic Excellence Scholarship

  • Merit-based financial support
  • Access to Cognira learning programs
  • Structured cognitive skill development
  • Mentorship and academic guidance
  • Digital learning resources
  • Progress tracking and evaluations

Creative & Applied Skills

Creative Talent Scholarship

  • Support for design and creative learners
  • Portfolio-based evaluation
  • Skill-focused learning tracks
  • Industry-aligned training modules
  • Access to creative tools and resources
  • Mentor feedback and improvement plans

Inclusive Education

Access & Support Scholarship

  • Need-based financial assistance
  • Affordable access to Cognira programs
  • Foundational concept strengthening
  • Career guidance and counseling
  • Continuous academic support
  • Equal learning opportunities
